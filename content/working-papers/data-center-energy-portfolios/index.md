abstract: "Data centers require electricity around the clock at high reliability, a demand profile that is today met largely by fossil generation. We develop a techno-economic optimization model that determines least-cost power portfolios for supplying data-center loads from solar, wind, natural gas, battery storage, grid electricity, and diesel generators under alternative reliability targets. The model co-optimizes technology sizing and full-year hourly operation, showing that the dispatch strategy is itself a major lever on both cost and reliability."
