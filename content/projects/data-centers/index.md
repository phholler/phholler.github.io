## A techno-economic optimization model

Data centers need electricity around the clock at high reliability — a demand profile that today is met largely with fossil generation. This project builds a techno-economic model for optimized power portfolios to find the least-cost way to supply different data-center loads from clean and dispatchable sources — solar, wind, gas, battery storage, grid, and diesel generators — under different reliability targets.

The core question goes beyond *what to build* to *how to run it*: the model co-optimizes technology sizing and full-year hourly operation, and shows that the operating strategy — how the system is dispatched hour by hour under real-world limits — is a major lever on both cost and reliability.

Joint work with A. Bach, R. Deryati, and Stefan Reichelstein. The underlying
working paper is [Optimal Energy Portfolios for Data Centers](/working-papers/data-center-energy-portfolios/).
